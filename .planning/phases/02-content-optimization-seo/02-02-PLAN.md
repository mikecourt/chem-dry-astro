# Plan 02-02: Internal Linking & Content Enhancement

## Overview

**Phase**: 2 - Content Optimization & SEO
**Plan**: 2 of 2
**Goal**: Implement strategic internal linking and enhance local content signals

**Estimated Duration**: 15-20 minutes
**Risk Level**: Low (additive content changes)

## Context

### Current State
- City pages link to generic "Nearby Cities" in service areas section
- No cross-linking between city pages in body content
- No links from city pages to service pages
- FAQ sections don't link to related content
- H2 headings don't consistently include city names

### Target State
- Strategic internal links in body content (2-3 per section)
- City-to-city cross-links in "Nearby Cities" sections
- City-to-service links in service cards
- FAQ answers link to relevant pages where appropriate
- H2 headings include city name for local relevance (2-3 per page)

### SEO Rationale

Internal linking:
- Distributes PageRank across city pages
- Helps search engines understand site structure
- Improves crawlability of deep pages
- Provides contextual relevance signals

## Tasks

### Task 1: Add City Cross-Links to Service Areas Section

**Files**: All 7 city pages in `src/pages/`

**Changes**:
Each city page should link to 2-3 nearby cities in the "Nearby Cities Served" section.

**Link Matrix**:
| City | Links To |
|------|----------|
| Phoenix | Mesa, Tempe, Scottsdale |
| Mesa | Gilbert, Tempe, Chandler |
| Gilbert | Mesa, Chandler, Queen Creek |
| Chandler | Gilbert, Tempe, Mesa |
| Tempe | Mesa, Scottsdale, Phoenix |
| Scottsdale | Phoenix, Tempe, Mesa |
| Queen Creek | Gilbert, Mesa, Chandler |

**Implementation**:
```astro

```

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] Each city page links to 2-3 neighboring cities
- [ ] Links use proper anchor text (city name)
- [ ] Links point to correct routes (/mesa, /gilbert, etc.)
- [ ] No self-referential links (Phoenix page doesn't link to Phoenix)

### Task 4: Add Contextual Links in FAQ Answers

**Files**: All 7 city pages in `src/pages/`

**Goal**: Add 1-2 internal links within FAQ answers where relevant.

**Opportunities**:
- "Do you clean upholstery?" → Link to `/services/upholstery-cleaning`
- "What about tile?" → Link to `/services/tile-grout-cleaning`
- "Do you serve [nearby city]?" → Link to that city page
- "How does your process work?" → Link to `/about` or `/services/carpet-cleaning`

**Implementation**:
```astro
<!-- In FAQ answer -->
Yes! We offer professional <a href="/services/upholstery-cleaning" class="text-primary hover:underline">upholstery cleaning</a> using the same safe, effective process.
```

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] Each city page has 1-2 contextual links in FAQ section
- [ ] Links are natural and helpful (not forced)
- [ ] Links use descriptive anchor text
