## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does it really take to dry?**
Most carpets are dry and ready for normal foot traffic in just 1-2 hours. That's about 80% faster than steam cleaning. Heavier-traffic areas or thicker carpets may take 3-4 hours, but you'll never wait 1-2 days like with traditional methods. This means less disruption to your day and no risk of mold or mildew growth.

**Do I need to move my furniture?**
We recommend moving small items, valuables, and breakables before we arrive. We'll move most furniture (sofas, chairs, tables, beds) and clean underneath, then place everything back on protective tabs to prevent moisture transfer while your carpets finish drying. Very heavy items like entertainment centers or dressers typically stay in place.

**Is the process used by Chem-Dry really safe for my kids and pets?**
Absolutely. The Natural®, our primary cleaning solution, is green-certified, non-toxic, and contains no soaps or harsh chemicals. It's safe for your entire family—even crawling babies and curious pets. There's no need to keep kids or pets off the carpets after cleaning.

**How often should I get my carpets professionally cleaned?**
Most carpet manufacturers recommend professional cleaning every 12-18 months to maintain your warranty and extend carpet life. For homes with pets, children, or allergy sufferers, we recommend cleaning every 6-9 months to maintain optimal indoor air quality and appearance.

**What about tough stains—can you really get them out?**
While we can't guarantee removal of every stain (some substances permanently dye carpet fibers), the process used by Chem-Dry removes most common stains effectively. We pre-treat problem areas with specialized products and use our Hot Carbonating Extraction to lift stains other cleaners leave behind. If we can't remove it, we'll be honest with you.

**Do you offer a satisfaction guarantee?**
Yes. If you're not completely satisfied with our cleaning, let us know within 30 days and we'll return to re-clean any areas of concern at no charge. Your satisfaction is our priority—that's the "white glove" promise.

**What areas do you serve?**
We proudly serve the entire Phoenix Metro area, including Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Scottsdale, Tempe, Queen Creek, Apache Junction, San Tan Valley, and surrounding communities. If you're in the East Valley, we've got you covered.

**How much does carpet cleaning cost?**
Every home is unique, which is why we provide free, no-obligation estimates. Pricing depends on the square footage, carpet condition, and any special treatments needed. We'll give you a clear, honest price before we start—no hidden fees or surprises.
