# Chem-Dry Official Advertising Rules and Guidelines

> **Effective February 2026**

The key to the success of Chem-Dry® is how well we tell our story, because when we tell our story well, we win! We need to provide consumers with clear and consistent, quality advertising messages across a wide spectrum of media. When we do, we can tell the Chem-Dry story more effectively and begin to give Chem-Dry a distinct, recognizable brand image. At the same time, we can entice consumers to respond to our messaging directly, resulting in an immediate boost in sales.

**These guidelines must be followed by all Chem-Dry franchises when advertising under the Chem-Dry brand name.**

The Franchise Agreement states that all advertising using the Chem-Dry name needs to be approved in writing by A BELFOR Company (herein referred to BFG). However, BFG does not require franchisees to submit all advertising for approval as long as the franchisee complies with all of the Advertising Rules and Guidelines. Therefore, all advertisements that meet the guidelines will be considered approved. *However, during the first 12 months of operation, it is required that all advertising be submitted to your State Representative in Franchise Administration for approval.*

The Advertising Rules and Guidelines apply to all forms of advertising and Branded Materials; e.g., business directories, direct mail pieces, newspaper, television, radio, digital marketing, coupon books or offers, place of business materials (doors, windows, signs...), vans, billboards, business cards, uniforms, invoices, and all other such advertising and Branded Materials herein - after all inclusively referred to as "Branded Materials". All Branded Materials must be professional in meaning and appearance, which is determined at the sole discretion of BFG, and in compliance with the Advertising Rules and Guidelines.

Franchisees are reminded that Branded Materials not in compliance with the Advertising Rules and Guidelines may subject their franchise to a fine of up to $2,000 and/or termination of their franchise. Please refer to your Franchise Agreement and the Fines Schedule for more details.

It is your responsibility to ensure that the Branded Material is correct before it is put into circulation. If a Branded Material is printed or aired and one or more of the guidelines have not been followed, the fine may be placed on your account, even if it is the mistake or oversight of the advertiser/representative.

Franchisees who have questions or wish to seek approval of a Branded Material may submit proposed Branded Materials to the Franchise Administration Department at BFG for approval.

Submitted Branded Materials will be reviewed for the correct use of trademarks and service marks and the general acceptability of the representations about the Chem-Dry system as a whole. Please allow at least 2-4 business days from the date we receive your Branded Material for our comments. Advertising Rules and Guidelines are updated periodically. Changes are generally announced in monthly marketing update emails and communicated in the Chem-Dry owner Facebook group.

Franchises with Branded Materials that violate these guidelines are subject not only to fines and/or termination, but may also be required to disconnect or re-route the phone number, email address and website URL used in the non-approved Branded Material.

## Section Six: Advertising Best Practices & Prohibited Actions

In order to ensure that any claim being made is truthful, accurate, and non-deceptive:

- Don't use outdated information
- Check currentness of all figures used in advertising materials
- Don't make a claim that is not backed by evidence
- Ensure that any objective claim (express or implied) has a reasonable basis before publishing such a claim
- Don't omit material facts in a claim, as this can be misleading
- A misleading statement, even if literally true, is deceptive and thus, prohibited
- While puffery is allowed in advertising, false or misleading statements of objective fact are prohibited
- Ask: would a reasonable consumer rely on this claim?
- If a disclaimer or disclosure is required, ensure that it is prominently placed close to the claim and easy to understand
- A disclaimer does not cure an otherwise false or deceptive advertisement

### Green Claims

In addition to the above, when making green claims:
- The claim must not be misleading to a reasonable consumer
- The claim must be substantiated by competent and reliable scientific evidence behind the claim with respect to both (1) people and (2) the environment

### Endorsements

- Like other advertising claims, endorsements must be honest and not misleading
- An endorsement must show the endorser's honest opinions, findings, beliefs, or experience
- It is prohibited to use an endorsement to make an unsubstantiated or misleading statement
- If a consumer is making an endorsement, their endorsement alone does not qualify as substantiation
- Independent substantiation is required, or if the endorser's experience is not representative of what consumers will generally achieve, the generally expected experience must be disclosed (and substantiated)
- If an endorser received payment or consideration for their endorsement, this fact should be disclosed
- Similarly, any material connection between the endorser and the company must be disclosed
