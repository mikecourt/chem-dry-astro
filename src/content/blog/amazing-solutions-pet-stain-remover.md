Pets are wonderful additions to any home, but accidents can leave unpleasant stains and odors indoors. Repeated use of the same spot can also leave the area unsanitary. These stains and odors can be difficult to remove. Fortunately, there are a few pet stain removers on the market that can help you quickly and effectively clean pet-accident stains.
![Cat Urine Stain Removal](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Cat-Urine-Stain-Removal.jpg)                                                              Cat Urine Stain Removal
One great option for pet stains is a[ steam cleaner](https://www.thespruce.com/best-steam-cleaners-4158672). This product works by forcing the cleaner’s liquid to reach deep inside the fabric or carpet fibers. As the solution agitates, it will lift the stain away. This stain remover is great for carpets, upholstery, stairs, and even cars. Another great pet stain remover is a baking soda mixture. It can break down tough stains that other products won’t touch. A baking soda and vinegar solution can effectively clean carpets and rugs. This homemade method can help pet owners get rid of stubborn pet stains. The solution is fresh for pets and will leave your carpet looking as good as new again. Another pet stain remover that you can make at home is a homemade stain and odor remover. It works well on mild stains like spaghetti sauce and can be stored in a spray bottle for quick cleaning. However, it is important to test the solution on an inconspicuous spot before using it on a carpeted floor. It’s possible that the mixture may end up discoloring the carpet.
