When you have your carpets cleaned, you may be tempted to leave a tip. However, it’s not customary to do so. You can leave the cleaners a check for a small tip or leave a note on their paperwork explaining that a small tip would be appreciated.
![](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Best-carpet-cleaning.jpg)Best carpet cleaning
While most contractors do not require tips, it’s always a good idea to give a small tip. A $20 or so can be appropriate. However, some [carpet professionals](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/professional-carpet-cleaner/) are reluctant to accept cash, so it’s best to check the business’s website or ask the owner about their tipping policy. Some business owners may not even require a tip and instead charge a rate commensurate to the value of the service. Even if you can’t afford to tip the first time, it’s important to recognize the hard work of these service providers. They may have cleaned your carpets and may have even moved furniture from another room. A small tip is a gesture of gratitude, but you don’t have to tip carpet cleaners to appreciate the hard work they do. The standard tip for a carpet cleaner is between five and twenty percent of their bill. However, if you have several carpets cleaned by the same professional, you can leave a larger tip.
