Keeping your area rugs clean is important for a variety of reasons. Whether you have antique or modern rugs, these rugs need to be cleaned regularly to keep them looking their best. Fortunately, there are a number of tips and tricks you can use to maintain the appearance of your rugs.

![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/63WBmeaF9nI/hqdefault.jpg)

 

First, vacuum the rug as often as possible. Area rugs are surprisingly good at absorbing dirt, so it’s important to vacuum them every day. You should also wash them regularly with a dishwashing soap solution. Then, let the rug dry completely before putting it back on the floor. In addition, you can use an unscented talcum powder to treat stains. When using a powder, be sure to follow the instructions on the container to ensure that it is fresh for your area rug.

For more thorough cleaning, consider hiring a professional. A professional can utilize specialized equipment and cleaning solutions that can get into the deeper layers of the carpet. In addition to a deep cleaning solution, a professional rug cleaner uses techniques such as immersion and hot water extraction to get rid of stains and dirt. After cleaning, the area rug is then allowed to dry in a temperature-controlled environment.
