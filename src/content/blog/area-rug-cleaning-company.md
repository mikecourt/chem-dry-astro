An area [rug cleaning company](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/) can help you maintain the beauty of your rugs. Rugs can become dirty from everyday use, and regular vacuuming may not be enough to remove stubborn stains and odors. Professional rug cleaners have years of experience and thorough knowledge of how to handle various stains and odors.
![](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/chemdry.jpg)ChemDry white glove carpet
It is important to remember that area rugs are not just functional pieces of decor – they are also home to bacteria, non-living allergens*, and insects. If not cleaned regularly, these contaminants can get trapped in the fiber, and can aggravate allergies and other illnesses. Professional cleaning can remove these contaminants and make your floors healthier for you and your family. Do not attempt to clean an area rug with household cleaning solutions as this can damage the fiber of the rug and can result in stains that won’t come out.

A professional cleaning can also extend the life of your area rugs. This type of service is ideal for rugs that cost a lot of money. The process can remove stubborn stains and restore the color of the rug to its original state. It will ensure that your area rugs look great and last for many years.

Professional rug cleaning professionals use environmentally friendly solutions that are specially designed for cleaning fibers. They combine plant-based cleaning products with enzymes and natural fragrances.
