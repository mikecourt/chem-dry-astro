Area rugs can be difficult to clean and often become stained and dirty. Many people choose to take them to a [professional rug cleaning](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/carpet-cleaner-reviews/) service to have them cleaned professionally. These services are affordable and convenient. Some of them also offer free pick up and delivery. This is a convenient option for people who don’t have the time to pick up the rug themselves.

![](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/6243972c6f052b5ed71590bf_techsmile2.png)

Brimley’s white glove Carpet ChemDry

Professional cleaning services do not leave soap behind, which can attract more dirt. They also use cleaners that will leave your rugs cleaner for a longer period of time. In addition, these services can match the right rug to your home’s interior decor. Having a professional clean your area rugs ensures a long lasting appearance and a healthier home. Some companies offer drop-off services for cleaning area rugs. However, others do not. This is because some do not have the proper equipment or expertise to properly clean them. Therefore, it’s important to ask before you choose a cleaning service. The cleaner should be willing to give you an estimate before performing any work.

While the on-site service is convenient, it is best to get a professional cleaner to do your area rugs. This way, you don’t have to worry about damaging your floors or the rugs. In addition, it is important to ensure that the rug is placed on a non-damaging surface before cleaning. For example, wood floors shouldn’t be used if you want to have your area rugs cleaned properly.
