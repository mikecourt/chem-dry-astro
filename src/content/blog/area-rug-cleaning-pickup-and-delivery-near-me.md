Area rugs tend to collect a lot of dust. A wool area rug measuring 8×10 can contain as much as 10 pounds of dust. This makes them one of the largest airborne filters in a home. Many people assume that vacuuming them is sufficient to keep them looking clean. However, doing this can cause damage to the carpet, which is why you need to get them professionally cleaned.
![](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Commercial-Carpet-Cleaning.jpg)Commercial Carpet Cleaning
In order to get the best possible results from an area rug cleaning, you must first know what to expect. A poorly cleaned area rug may become unpleasant and gritty, and you may have to replace it altogether. However, professional area rug cleaning can help you keep your home looking its best. This will ensure that your rugs last longer and impress guests.

Rug cleaning prices depend on the size and material of the rug. A standard eight by ten foot area rug will cost around $150 to $500. However, smaller rugs can cost anywhere from $60 to $300. Moreover, you may have to pay extra for repairs, such as reweaving, blocking, and straightening. These additional services can add up to $200 to $500 per square foot. You may also consider hiring an carbonated company to clean your rug. These companies use natural cleaning agents. These agents are less likely to cause damage to your area rug, especially if it is handmade. The best companies will also have a technician who knows the proper techniques to clean your rugs.
