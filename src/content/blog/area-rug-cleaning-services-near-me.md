Because of this, it is important to regularly clean them. This will improve their performance and appearance. Additionally, they can act as giant air filters that trap dust, dirt, and non-living allergens*. Those inhaled particles can lead to asthma attacks, coughing, and skin rashes.

It is essential to clean your area rugs on a regular basis. Without proper cleaning, they can become gritty and uncomfortable. Getting them professionally cleaned will ensure that they are clean and fresh for your family to enjoy. A professional cleaner will use cleaning techniques that won’t damage your rug’s fibers or colors.
