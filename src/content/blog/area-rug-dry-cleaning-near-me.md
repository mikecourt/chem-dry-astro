Area rugs, especially oriental rugs, can be beautiful and valuable additions to any room. However, they can also quickly become dirty, especially if they’re exposed to a large amount of traffic. To maintain the vibrancy of these rugs, it’s important to regularly clean them. A professional rug cleaning service can restore them to their original, vibrant color and pattern. The technicians at [Brimley’s White Glove Chem-Dry](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/)  have the experience and the proper equipment to clean all types of rugs.![](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/sss.png)

Rugs need to be cleaned regularly to maintain their beauty and prevent physical damage. Without the right cleaning, they can become dirty, gritty, and uncomfortable. You may even need to seek out special repair services if there’s any damage. Fortunately, area rug cleaning is not as hard as it may seem. If you’re doing the cleaning yourself, you can use a garden hose and buckets of water to remove the dirt and stains. Once the cleaning solution is applied, make sure to leave the rug to dry in the sun or over a railing. If possible, use a fan to speed up the drying process.

Professionals who clean area rugs with hot water extraction use an advanced cleaning process to remove even the dirtiest stains. The hot water used in the process is fresh for area rugs and will not damage them. This method of cleaning is often recommended by carpet manufacturers and will help to maintain the beauty and longevity of your carpet and rugs for a long time.
