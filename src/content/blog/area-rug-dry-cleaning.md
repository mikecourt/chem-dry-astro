There are many [techniques](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o_xG5KyhLi4) that can be used to clean rugs. The first step is to test the rug’s condition. You can do this by lifting the corner or kicking it back. You can also try running your hand along the face of the rug in a circular motion. If you see a layer of dirt or grit on the palm of your hand, then your rug is probably dirty.
![Carpet Cleaning](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Carpet-Cleaning-1.jpg)Carpet Cleaning
Taking care of your area rug will ensure that it retains its beauty and functionality. If not cleaned properly, it will become uncomfortable and gritty. You can enjoy it for years to come with the proper care. Whether you have a hand-tufted Persian runner in your hallway, or an industrial mat in the kitchen, you’ll want to keep it looking its best. When you’re ready to entertain, you should make sure that your area rug is clean and healthy. A dry cleaning professional will know how to clean all types of rugs and will do it in the proper way for the best results. Moreover, you’ll save money by not having to wait for a couple of weeks.

If you’re cleaning your own rug, use only a small amount of detergent. It’s best to test a small area first to make sure the detergent will not damage the rug. You should also try the cleaning solution on the back of the rug to see if the colors run. If the dyes run, you’ll want to take it to a professional. You can also use a soft bristle brush to get rid of any spots or stains that have already occurred.
