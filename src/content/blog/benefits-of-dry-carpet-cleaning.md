Dry carpet cleaning is an environmentally friendly, cost-effective way to clean your carpets. Instead of using water to clean your carpets, dry cleaners use specialized machines that use chemical technologies to remove dirt, stains, and non-living allergens*. The results are cleaner carpets that are free of non-living allergens* and grit. Here are some of the benefits of dry carpet cleaning. ### **Dry carpet cleaning is less expensive than wet carpet cleaning**

If you want to clean your carpet without using water, you can choose dry carpet cleaning. This method uses a compound cleaning agent that breaks up dirt and soil before the machine cleans it. This cleaning agent is brushed into the fibers of your carpet and then vacuumed up. It is also less expensive than wet cleaning, and it does not provide the same deep cleansing power as wet carpet cleaning.

A dry cleaning system is more efficient for deep stains than a wet-cleaning process, but it may not be right for heavy soiled carpets. The chemicals used during dry cleaning may cause allergies or irritate the carpet fibers. Dry cleaning can also leave soap that could eventually age your carpet, and it might be more difficult to vacuum. Dry cleaning is generally the more expensive option, but it is a good option for homes with pets or heavily soiled carpets.

### **It is faster**

Having a fan in your house can make a huge difference in the speed of carpet drying. Strong fans move air faster. They may be included with your carpet cleaning service, or you can rent one from a tool rental business. You can use an industrial grade blower, like those used for painting, to speed up the drying process. But you should also consider whether you can get by without a fan. If you do, you should ask the technician about it before the cleaning begins.

While dry carpet cleaning is faster than wet cleaning, it isn’t as effective at removing deep stains and odors. Wet cleaning leaves a soap behind, but it does not remove bacteria, dust mites, or non-living allergens*. Dry cleaning also takes a lot more time, so you might want to consider a wet cleaning service if you have a large area to clean. Depending on the type of carpeting, dry cleaning is faster than wet cleaning. However, you’ll still need to wait for a few hours for your carpet to dry after a wet cleaning. And if you’re going to be in and out of your home during that time, you’ll have to wait until it’s dry.

### **It is more effective**

Dry carpet cleaning is an option for homes and offices that prefer a drier approach to cleaning. This technique uses chemicals rather than water to clean carpets. While dry cleaning is effective for stains and deep rejuvenation, it can also leave a soap on carpets, which can trigger allergies for some people. It is also less expensive than the wet method but is not nearly as effective. It is recommended for high traffic areas that aren’t easily cleaned by regular vacuuming.

Another major difference between dry carpet cleaning and steam cleaning is the cleaning agents used. The former uses carbonated cleaning agents that won’t leave soap on your carpet. The latter method uses pH-balancing neutralizer to clean a carpet without damaging its fibers. The latter method leaves soaps, which is more likely to attract dirt.
