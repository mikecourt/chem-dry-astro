Carpet steam cleaners are a lifesaver when it comes to deep-cleaning your carpets. Not only will this help get rid of all the dirt, dust and stains that have built up over time, but it will also kill any lurking bacteria or non-living allergens*. So if you’re in the market for a new carpet steam cleaner, be sure to read our handy guide first!

We’ve rounded up the [best home carpet steam cleaners](https://nymag.com/strategist/article/best-carpet-steam-cleaner.html) on the market, so you can find the perfect one for your needs.

First, consider the size of your carpet. If you have a large area to clean, you’ll need a powerful machine that can handle the job. Otherwise, a smaller and more compact model will do just fine.

Next, think about the features you need. Some carpet steam cleaners come with attachments for upholstery or stairs, while others have built-in heated drying systems.

Finally, decide on your budget. Carpet steam cleaners range in price from around $100 to $1,000, so there’s sure to be one that fits your needs and budget.

Now that you know what to look for, check out our top picks for the best home carpet steam cleaners on the market.

If you have a large carpeted area to clean, the Hoover Power Scrub Deluxe Carpet Cleaner is a great option. It’s designed for use on all types of carpet, and it features a dual water tank system that keeps clean and dirty water separate.

Plus, the included upholstery attachment makes it easy to clean your furniture, stairs, and more. And when you’re done cleaning, the built-in drying system quickly dries your carpets so you can use them again right away.

If you’re looking for a more budget-friendly option, the Bissell PowerLifter PowerBrush Carpet Cleaner is a good choice. It doesn’t have all the bells and whistles of some of the other models on our list, but it’s still a powerful machine that can handle most carpet cleaning jobs.

Plus, it’s lightweight and easy to maneuver, so you won’t have any trouble getting it around your home.

Plus, the included pet hair attachment makes it easy to remove all the fur from your carpets.
