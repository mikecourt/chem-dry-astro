One of the best ways to remove pet vomit stains is to use a vinegar solution. Simply mix equal parts white vinegar and water and apply it to the stain using a clean cloth or sponge. Let it sit for about 15 minutes, then blot it up with a dry cloth. You may need to repeat this process a few times to completely remove the stain.

If vinegar doesn’t seem to be doing the trick, you can also try using baking soda. Just make a paste by mixing together baking soda and water and apply it to the affected area. Let it sit for about 30 minutes, then blot it up with a clean cloth.

If you’re still having trouble removing the stain, you can try using a commercial pet vomit stain remover. There are plenty of products on the market that are specifically designed to remove pet stains, so you should be able to find one that works well for your needs.
