You should consider the type of rug you have, the amount of traffic it receives and how often it needs to be cleaned before making your decision.

If you have a light-colored or delicate rug, you might want to consider a dry cleaner. Dry cleaners use solvents to clean rugs and are less likely to damage them than other types of cleaners.

If you have a heavy-traffic area, you might want to consider a steam cleaner. Steam cleaners use hot water to clean rugs and can remove more dirt and stains than other types of cleaners.

If you have an area rug that is made of natural fibers, you might want to consider a shampooer. Shampooers use detergents to clean rugs and are gentle enough to use on delicate fibers.

When choosing a rug cleaner, be sure to read the labels carefully. Some cleaners are designed for use on specific types of rugs and might damage other types of rugs.

Once you’ve chosen a rug cleaner, be sure to follow the directions carefully. Some cleaners require you to pre-treat stains before using the cleaner, while others recommend that you vacuum the rug first.

When using any type of cleaner, be sure to protect your hands and clothing. Wear gloves and old clothes that you don’t mind getting dirty.

Be sure to test the cleaner on a small area of the rug before using it on the entire rug. This will help you make sure that the cleaner is fresh to use on your rug.

If you have any questions about using a rug cleaner, be sure to ask the salesperson at the store. They should be able to give you all the information you need to make an informed decision.
