Not only does it make you feel good when you walk in the door, but it also helps protect your health. One of the most important areas of the home to keep clean is the sofa. Sofas can easily become stained and dirty, and they can also be a breeding ground for non-living allergens* and other contaminants. That’s why it’s important to have a good sofa cleaner on hand. There are many different [types of sofa cleaners](https://whywelikethis.com/best-sofa-fabric-cleaner-products/) available today, so it can be difficult to decide which one is right for you.

Here is a brief overview of some of the most popular sofa cleaners available today.

 	- Upholstery Cleaner

An upholstery cleaner is a must-have for any sofa owner. These cleaners are designed to clean all types of upholstery, including fabric and leather. They can remove dirt, dust, and stains from your sofa, leaving it looking like new. Upholstery cleaners usually come in a spray bottle, so they’re easy to use. Just spray the cleaner on the affected area and wipe it off with a clean cloth.

 	- Steam Cleaner

A steam cleaner is another great option for cleaning your sofa. Steam cleaners use hot water and steam to clean and disinfect surfaces. They’re very effective at removing dirt, dust, and stains. Steam cleaners are usually handheld and come with a variety of attachments to help you reach all the nooks and crannies of your sofa.

 	- Foam Cleaner

Foam cleaners are similar to upholstery cleaners, but they’re specifically designed for cleaning leather sofas. These cleaners are applied as a foam and then wiped off with a clean cloth. Foam cleaners can remove dirt, dust, and stains from your leather sofa, leaving it looking like new.

 	- Liquid Cleaner

Liquid cleaners are another option for cleaning your sofa. These cleaners come in a spray bottle and are applied directly to the affected area. Liquid cleaners can remove dirt, dust, and stains from your sofa, leaving it looking like new.

 	- Dry Cleaner

Dry cleaners are a great option for those who don’t want to use water or steam to clean their sofa. Dry cleaners are applied as a powder and then brushed into the affected area. They can remove dirt, dust, and stains from your sofa, leaving it looking like new.

No matter what type of sofa cleaner you choose, be sure to follow the instructions carefully. This will help ensure that your sofa is cleaned properly and that you don’t damage it in the process.
