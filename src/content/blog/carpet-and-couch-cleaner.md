A [carpet and couch cleaner](https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter/reviews/best-portable-carpet-and-upholstery-cleaner/) is a useful device for cleaning carpets and couches. There are several types of carpet cleaners, but it’s best to choose the right one for your needs. A good carpet cleaner should have an upholstery attachment. Depending on the type of fabric, you may need to use a special cleaning solution to get the best results.

If you don’t know what type of cleaning product to use, it’s better to hire an expert. A technician from a carpet and couch cleaning company can recommend the right solution for your particular stains. You should also check the manufacturer’s recommendations before attempting to clean your upholstery on your own.

A steam carpet and couch cleaner is an excellent choice for cleaning carpets and couches. This type of carpet cleaner uses steam and heat to clean the fabric. The machine sucks up dirty water, then uses hot steam to clean the fabric. Its dual DirtLifter PowerBrush can remove stubborn stains.

A portable upholstery cleaner is another option for cleaning couches and other pieces of upholstery. These devices are lightweight and designed for spot-specific cleaning. They come with a rubber nub tool to give the material extra agitation.
