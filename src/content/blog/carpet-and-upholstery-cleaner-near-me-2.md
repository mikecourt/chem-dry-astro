When choosing a carpet and upholstery cleaner, you need to know the type of fiber your carpet is made of. Most commercial carpets are made of nylon, but some are a blend of two fibers. This is important to know because improper cleaning can damage the fiber and cause it to change color and texture. Your [cleaner](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/) should also be able to groom your carpet for a more lustrous finish. Some clients may also request a deodorizing or antimicrobial treatment. You should also know that a professional cleaner can keep your wool wall to wall rugs clean and healthy, and can maintain their luster and color.

 

The carpet and upholstery cleaning process includes deep cleaning of the fibers and thorough rinse-out of cleaning solutions and soil. After the cleaning process, you should have your carpet treated with Scotch Guard to help keep it looking newer longer. The cleaner should also use a carpet protector to prevent soil from coming back.
