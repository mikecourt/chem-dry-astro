There are many types of commercial carpet cleaner extractors on the market today. Choosing the right one for your company’s needs can be tricky. You want one that will allow workers to get the most carpeted space clean in the least amount of time. Companies such as Imperial Dade and EBP can help you select the right one for your needs.

When using a carpet cleaner extractor, you start at one corner of the carpet and walk backwards. The extractor has three switches that spray the cleaning solution onto the carpet and spin the brush to scrub it. You will need to wait for the carpet to dry before you place furniture back on it. The process of drying the carpet is not difficult, but it is time-consuming.

In addition to stain removal, a carpet extractor is capable of cleaning a wide variety of carpeting types. The carpeting material and density of the fibers will dictate the type of cleaning solution you need. The temperature of the cleaning solution is also an important factor. Too hot of a solution will shrink natural fibres. If you are purchasing a carpet cleaner extractor, make sure to read the instructions carefully to make sure it’s the right temperature for the carpet you have. In addition to the cleaning solution, a carpet cleaner extractor can also be powered by a powerful vacuum motor. The latter is ideal for cleaning large areas. They are usually the most powerful models, but they are also the bulkiest. The latter can be tricky to store or transport.
