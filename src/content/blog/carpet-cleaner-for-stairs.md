Before beginning, make sure to empty all of the water from the stairs and then use a [carpet cleaner for stairs](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/shop/carpet-cleaner-for-stairs). To clean the stairs, start from the top and work your way down the stairs, working from left to right. If possible, you can use a wet/dry vacuum cleaner to remove the excess moisture. If this is not possible, you can also use absorbent cloths or towels. But that process can be tedious and time-consuming, so it’s best to use a carpet cleaner.

When choosing a carpet cleaner for stairs, it’s important to consider how many stairs you have in your home. You might have a single flight of stairs, or multiple flights. If you have multiple flights of stairs, you may want to choose a unit that is lightweight. Also, consider the size of the vacuum head. Some models are smaller and easier to handle than others.

If you’re worried about the weight of the cleaner, there are corded and cordless models available. A cordless model, for example, can be useful for cleaning stairs.
