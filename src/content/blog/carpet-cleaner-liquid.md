Some of them are designed for use in carpet cleaning machines, while others can be applied by hand. Most of these cleaners work by applying the liquid to the stain and allowing it to sit for a specified time before wiping up. They also come in spray and foam form. Some are specifically made for removing stains caused by red wine or sticky substances.

Many carpet cleaner liquids have a strong scent. If you have sensitive skin or have children, it’s a good idea to use a scent-free cleaner. Other brands contain chemicals that may irritate your skin. In any case, you should follow the manufacturer’s instructions before using carpet cleaner liquid.

One type of carpet cleaner liquid is an ammonia solution. The formula is designed to break down blood and urine crystals that have set in the fibers of your carpet. Another kind of liquid is a mixture of dish washing soap and water. This is ideal for carpets that are not colorfast. Alternatively, you can use white vinegar. The mixture should be at least one part vinegar and two parts water.

A good carpet cleaner liquid should have a pH level of 10 or higher. This makes it alkaline and readily binds to acids in organic materials. Before applying carpet cleaner liquid, make sure your carpets are thoroughly vacuumed. You may also want to pre-treat heavily stained spots.
