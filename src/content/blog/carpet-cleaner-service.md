### Choosing a carpet cleaner

A simple method of choosing a carpet cleaning company is to look online for reviews. Many companies post reviews on their websites but not all are genuine. If you see a lot of complaints from previous customers, chances are they’re not very good. Instead, check out reviews written by current or previous customers. Customers will often share details of their experiences, as well as the things they would change if they could do it again. You should read these reviews to find out which companies have good reputations and are not afraid to share them.

Having a list of your needs will help you narrow down your search. Look for a company that’s local and offers a range of services. Check the cleaner’s website for testimonials from previous clients. Check for insurance and licenses, too. Make sure the company you choose is licensed, insured, and bonded. This means they’ve undergone background checks and are prepared to cover any damage that may occur to your carpets.
