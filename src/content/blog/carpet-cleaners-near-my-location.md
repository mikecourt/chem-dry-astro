Before you decide to[ hire a carpet cleaner](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/), ask them about their cleaning techniques. Most professionals use several different methods to clean carpets, and some are better suited for certain types of carpets than others. Choosing the right method can affect the pile length, overall condition, and amount of staining.

When it comes to the cost of carpet cleaning, the amount varies from person to person. Some companies charge by the square foot, while others require you to pay extra for additional services. For example, if you have a damaged couch or a heavy chair that needs to be removed, you’ll be charged an extra fee of $200. You can also save money by preparing the room before the carpet cleaners arrive. If possible, explain to them the areas in the room that receive high traffic and the stains that require professional attention.

Steam cleaning is another popular method for cleaning carpets. It dries in about one to two hours and uses hot water with a carbonating agent. This method is highly effective at killing germs and non-living allergens*. However, it is not recommended for heavily soiled carpets. It can cost anywhere from $75 to $350.
