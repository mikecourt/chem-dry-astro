### Steam cleaning

Whether you live in a high-traffic area like an office or a family with sensitive smells, steam cleaning is the ideal solution. The water vapor produced during steam cleaning is non-odorous, and it is also effective at lifting dirt and bacteria that contribute to unpleasant smells. Steam cleaning will not inject any chemicals or other substances into the carpet fibers. It can be an effective way to restore a carpet that has lost its appearance because of wear and tear or because of poor maintenance.

Compared to traditional cleaning methods, steam cleaning is an excellent option for high-traffic areas. Because steam can penetrate deep into the carpet fibers, it is effective in removing dirt and non-living allergens*. Professional steam cleaners will use a high-pressure cleaning machine to extract the dirt and moisture from carpets. Once the carpet has been cleaned, it will look and smell like new again. You will be impressed by how much better your carpet will look.

### Dry cleaning

When you need a deep clean, a dry cleaning service may be the best solution. A dry cleaning system uses no water or moisture to deep clean your carpet. Instead of water, the cleaners use dry compounds or solutions. They are quicker and less labor intensive than wet cleaning. This method starts by using a low-moisture foaming system that separates dirt from the fibers. A vacuum is then used to extract the compound and clean your carpet thoroughly. Some dry cleaning solutions leave behind soap, so you may need to do another cleaning step in the future.

### Bonnet cleaning

Bonnet cleaning is an adaption of the process of hard floor spray buffing. It consists of a cotton, rayon, or polypropylene pad that is rubbed across the carpet by a rotary shampoo machine. This process removes soil and stains by agitating the carpet. A bonnet is designed to catch a majority of the dirt. The method is inexpensive, but not recommended in general.

Many carpet cleaning companies offer multiple cleaning methods, including hot water extraction. But some companies specialize in one type of cleaning. For example, some may use an organic cleaning solution. Bonnet cleaning uses a scrubbing pad to clean the surface of carpeting, while dry-cleaning is often done with powders. Bonnet cleaning, also known as spin bonnet cleaning, is similar to dry cleaning, but it focuses on the surface of the carpet. Unlike steam cleaning, bonnet cleaning machines use a rotating pad soaked in a cleaning solution.
