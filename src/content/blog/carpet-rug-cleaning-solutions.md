Cleaning your carpet can be a daunting task. Some messes are hard to remove, while others may be permanent. Depending on the type of stain, you might need a specialty solution. Fortunately, there are many brands available for you to choose from. These [products](https://www.amazon.com/Best-Carpet-Cleaning-Solution/s?k=Best+Carpet+Cleaning+Solution) will keep your carpet fresh and clean in between professional cleanings.

Some of these solutions are fresh for your carpet and can be made yourself. A popular brand contains yeast and botanical oils that will remove stains. You can pre-treat the stain yourself with this spray or apply it directly to the stain. You can also find natural carpet cleaning solutions online. These options are great if you’re a do-it-yourselfer or don’t want to spend a fortune on professional cleaning.

Another great solution for carpet & rug cleaning is a mixture of baking soda and vinegar. These two ingredients react to form a gas that will help lift the stain from your carpet. Pour one cup of each on the stain and rub it into the carpet. Leaving the mixture on for a few hours will allow the solution to do its work.

Another option for cleaning your carpet is a concentrated carpet shampoo. A concentrated shampoo will remove stubborn stains and remove pet odors. It is fresh to use in any area with water, and is compatible with septic and gray water. It is also a natural deodorizer that contains grapefruit seed and orange essence.
