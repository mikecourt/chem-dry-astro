Investing in a [carpet & upholstery cleaner](https://www.amazon.com/Carpet-Upholstery-Cleaners-Accessories/b?ie=UTF8&node=11333721011) is an excellent way to extend the life of your home’s soft surfaces. While vacuuming and steam cleaning your upholstery at home are great options, they aren’t enough. If you want to save money and get a deep clean, you need to hire a professional. At Rainbow International, we provide residential carpet and upholstery cleaning.

When choosing the right carpet & upholstery cleaner for your needs, make sure you choose one that is fresh for the environment. The best options are chemical-free, pet-friendly, and allergy-friendly. If you have children or pets at home, you will want to look for a cleaner that is fresh for them as well.

When using upholstery cleaners, you must be sure to test the cleaning spray to make sure it is fresh for your upholstery. It is a good idea to use a test patch first to make sure that the cleaning agent will not cause any damage to your upholstered items. Typically, upholstered items come with a code that shows the proper cleaning agent for them. Additionally, each machine has specific best practices and directions for use.

Upholstery cleaners also offer similar benefits to carpet cleaning, and can help improve the quality of indoor air in the home. They remove dirt, non-living allergens*, and dust. However, the effectiveness of upholstery cleaners depends on several factors, including your household habits and the number of people living in the home. For example, if you have a pet, it is advisable to have your upholstery cleaned twice a year. If you do not have a pet, however, it is recommended that you get your upholstery cleaned once a year.
