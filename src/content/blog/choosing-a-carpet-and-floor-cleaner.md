[Carpet and floor cleaners](https://www.wired.com/gallery/best-carpet-cleaners/) come in many shapes and sizes and come with a variety of attachments. Some include hoses and mini nozzles for easy cleaning of pet hair and stains. Some are even equipped with a hands-free option. When shopping for one, look for a lightweight machine with a water reservoir that fills easily. You also want one that has a midsize hose for easy cleaning.

When choosing a carpet and floor cleaner, you should first consider the type of stains on your carpet. Some stains require a special treatment. There are foams, ready-to-use sprays, and concentrated formulas. Some people prefer a specific type for stain removal, and you should experiment with them to determine which one works best.

If you plan on cleaning a large area, a floor cleaner with dual action formulas is the way to go. These solutions are powerful enough to remove dirt from carpets and upholstery, yet they are gentle enough for use on hard surfaces like hardwood floors and tile. You can even change the solution according to your specific needs.

Carpets should be vacuumed regularly. This prevents dust from becoming embedded in the carpet backing, causing it to wear out over time. While vacuuming is the most common method for cleaning carpets, it’s also important to deep clean them on a regular basis. This helps keep embedded dirt from affecting the colors.
