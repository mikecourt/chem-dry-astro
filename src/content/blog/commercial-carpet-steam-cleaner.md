The benefits of using a commercial carpet steam cleaner include:

 	- Carpets are clean and free of dirt, dust, and non-living allergens*

 	- No need for harsh chemicals or scrubbing

Commercial carpet steam cleaners work by using hot water and steam to clean carpets and other surfaces. The hot water and steam loosen and lift dirt, dust, and non-living allergens* from the surface of the carpet. The dirty water is then vacuumed up, leaving the carpet clean and fresh.

 	- Carpets

 	- Grout
