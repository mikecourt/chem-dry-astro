### Mesquite Groves Aquatic Center

Desert Breeze Park is filled with tall palms and shady mesquite trees that provide a cool respite from the summer heat. In addition to sports fields, playgrounds, and a community fishing lake, the park also features several picnic ramadas.

Mesquite Groves Aquatic Center is a municipal waterpark that has something for everyone. Visitors can swim in the lazy river, dive into one of two 1m diving boards, or slide down the water slides.

There’s even a family play pool and SCS interactive water feature with a giant tipping bucket. If you’d rather take it easy, you can ride the park’s train around the grounds.

### Sweeties of Arizona

When it comes to fun things to do in Chandler, this city offers a wide range of activities that are suitable for both children and adults. For example, golf lovers can indulge in a round of golf at the Bear Creek Golf Complex.

Sweeties Candy of Arizona is the largest candy store in Arizona, and it has everything from old-time favorites to a wall filled with Pez dispensers featuring pop culture characters like Stuart from Minions and Chase from Paw Patrol. They also have 350 styles of soda, including many made with real sugar and sold in glass bottles. This is a must-see for anyone with an adventurous sweet tooth.

### Chandler Bike Park

Chandler Bike Park is an outdoor recreational park that offers a wide variety of activities for people of all ages. It features a refreshing spray pad, a community fishing lake, tennis courts, sports fields, and walking trails. It also has a playground and a carousel that offer fun for families.

The bike park is a dream come true for biking enthusiasts. Its bike trails have varying levels of difficulty and offer thrilling rides for experienced riders. There are also several other attractions, including a jump box, molehill, and Hubba ledge.

### Merchant Square

There is no shortage of fun activities to enjoy at this city park. Its tall palm trees and shady mesquite provide respite from the summer heat. It also offers sports fields, a community fishing lake, three playgrounds, and a train ride. It even has five ramadas that are perfect for picnics.

This modern museum features multiple exhibitions, from in-house shows about Chandler’s history to national traveling exhibits focusing on art and culture. It also hosts a variety of classes and events for all ages.

### The Ostrich

There are a lot of activities and attractions at this park. There’s a beautiful lake, walking trails a playground, a train, and a carousel. You’ll want to bring quarters for the rides.

Ostriches can run up to 40 miles per hour and can kick with a force strong enough to kill a lion. They can also lie low, stretching their necks along the ground so that they look like they’re buried in the sand.

The Highgate Ostrich Farm offers many tours throughout the day, allowing you to soak in nature and learn about these giant birds.
