### **Pump track**

A 35-acre bike park with big jumps and scenic views is sure to be a hit with bikers and non-bikers alike. This facility has both big jumps and a pump track for the seasoned rider. A variety of riding styles is catered to every level. Beginners to advanced riders can take advantage of the bike park’s beginner lessons. Whether you’re looking to improve your skills or just want to test your limits, the pump track has it all.

The Pump track at the Desert Trails Bike Park is one of the city’s premier bike parks. It’s open from sunrise to sunset. If it’s raining or muddy, the park closes. The park has 30 parking spaces and two picnic tables. There’s a full bathroom and two tables for eating. And if you’re feeling daring, you can try the pump track’s mini-multitrack, which is shaped like a figure-8.
### **Skills track**

The Skills track at the Desert Trails Bike Park is one of the city’s best kept secrets. This bike park offers a variety of challenges including a pump track, a kids skills track, and a BMX jump course. The park includes restrooms and two ramadas with picnic tables. The track was designed in consultation with Mesa’s community and incorporates custom details to reduce the impact on the surrounding desert environment.

The skills track is made up of wooden features and a PRO vert section. The track also has a street plaza inspired section. The bike park also has four miles of moderately difficult single track, including rock drops, wooden features, and a pump track. The skills track is located in the middle of the park. While it is not quite as challenging as the pump track, it is still fun for beginners and intermediate riders alike.
