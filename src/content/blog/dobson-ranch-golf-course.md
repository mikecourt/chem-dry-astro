### **Dobson Ranch is an 18-hole championship golf course**

Dobson Ranch is an 18-hole golf course on Dobson Rd Mesana, Arizona. This public golf course features tree-lined fairways and a pro shop. The municipal course offers lighted driving ranges and a full-service golf pro shop. You can also rent golf clubs at the pro shop. After a round, take time to relax with a beverage and a snack.

The city of Mesa has opened an 18-hole public golf course at Dobson Ranch, just outside Phoenix. It has mature trees, large greens, and a driving range. There are also discounts for afternoon play. Dobson Ranch is a good option if you want to practice your short game. If you aren’t an advanced golfer, you can take advantage of the discount rate for early morning and late afternoon rounds.

The golf course is home to many challenging holes. The first is the 4th, a medium-length par three with water and bunkers on both sides of the green. The next hole is a dogleg left that can make a long hitter’s life difficult. Depending on where the pin is, an uphill putt will be easier to control.
### **It is ranked as the Best Municipal Golf Course in Arizona by Golf Digest**

The 18-hole course at this tree-lined municipal golf center is home to some of the best facilities in the state. The course offers lighted driving ranges and a pro shop. Golfers can enjoy the scenic desert views and relax in the pro shop before or after a game. Golfers will find a wide range of equipment and accessories to suit any skill level.

If you love to play golf, then you’ll find no better course than Dobson Ranch. The course has a par of 72 and is one of the most popular municipal courses in the area. It’s challenging and hosts 90,000 rounds each year. The course is easily accessible from Tucson. The Dobson Road exit is just south of Baseline Road. ### **It is near a major highway**

Located near a major highway, Dobson Ranch Golf Course is easy to access and offers ample parking. There’s a small pro shop, but the staff is friendly and helpful. The course is open all year round, but you should allow four to five hours to complete a round. The course has excellent conditioning and is affordable. Those who are new to the game can practice their skills on the course’s large practice bunker.

As a family-oriented community, Dobson Ranch is lacking in shopping and dining options. However, dining options are available on the outskirts. The Angry Crab Shack offers a Cajun twist on the traditional crab-bag boil. At this place, customers choose their seafood and seasonings, and then build their own boil bag.
