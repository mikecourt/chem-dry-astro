### Bike and Skate Park

Located in Chandler, Espee Park is home to a kid’s playground, baseball fields, and a bike and skate park. It also offers several recreational amenities, including an archery range and a dog park.

The bike and skate park at Espee Park Chandler AZ is designed for BMX bikes and was opened in 2007. It features many different elements to challenge riders of all skill levels. The facility includes a jump box, double roller, molehill, and hubba ledge. It also has a sub-rail and a vertical surface that’s taller than a basketball hoop.

This bike park is the perfect place for kids and teens to get their heart pumping while having fun with friends. It’s a great way to get them out of the house and away from their electronic devices. This park is conveniently situated near Alta Chandler at the Park, making it an ideal option for families. This park also has designated restrooms, which are kept clean and well-maintained.

### Baseball Fields

When visiting Chandler, AZ, baseball fans should be sure to check out Cactus League spring training, which usually runs from mid-February through March. The city is home to 15 Major League Baseball teams’ spring training grounds, including the Chicago Cubs.

The city also has a number of area parks that are great places to play and relax. For example, Tumbleweed Park offers walking paths around a tranquil lake, plus public tennis and sand volleyball courts. The Arizona Railway Museum, on the other hand, is a must-visit for train lovers. Visitors can climb aboard select passenger cars and a caboose to explore the historic railroad equipment.

The Chandler Center for the Arts is another great place to catch local and touring performances. The venue is unique in that it has 3 distinct performance spaces that break away from each other – this allows for multiple performances to take place at the same time without sound carrying across the different areas. Families with kids should consider the Chandler Crayola Experience, a unique hands-on attraction that takes crayons to the next level.
