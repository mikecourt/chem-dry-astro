Most people don’t think about upholstery cleaning until there’s a problem. And by then, it can be difficult to clean the dirt and stains that have built up over time. The good news is that upholstery cleaning doesn’t have to be difficult-or expensive!

There are a number of different upholstery cleaners on the market, but many of them contain harsh chemicals that can damage your furniture. luckily, there are a number of fresh and effective [DIY upholstery cleaners](https://www.thespruce.com/homemade-upholstery-cleaner-5073251) that you can make at home.

One of the best things about DIY upholstery cleaners is that they’re often made with natural ingredients. This means they’re fresh for all types of furniture, and they won’t damage your belongings in any way.

Another great thing about DIY upholstery cleaners is that they’re affordable to make. You can save money by making your own cleaner instead of buying expensive store-bought cleaners. Making your own upholstery cleaner is a great way to save money and avoid harsh chemicals. This recipe uses natural ingredients that are fresh for all types of upholstery-including sofas, chairs, and car seats!

What You’ll Need:

 	- 1/2 cup baking soda

 	- 1/4 cup vinegar

 	- 1 cup warm water

 	- Spray bottle

Instructions:

 	- Mix together the baking soda, vinegar, and water in the spray bottle. Shake well to combine.

 	- To use, simply spray the cleaner onto your upholstery and scrub with a brush or sponge.

 	- Rinse with clean water and allow to air dry.

So, if you’re looking for an affordable, fresh, and easy-to-use upholstery cleaner, give this recipe a try!
