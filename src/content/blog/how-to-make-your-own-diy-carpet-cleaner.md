Not only is this unsightly, but it can also be dangerous for your family if dirt and dust are allowed to build up. A [DIY carpet cleaner](https://www.thespruce.com/homemade-carpet-cleaner-recipes-1388729) is an easy way to keep your carpets looking new longer. This cleaner is made with simple ingredients that you may already have in your home, and it requires no special skills or equipment to make.

To make your own DIY carpet cleaner, simply mix the following ingredients together in a bowl:

1 cup of warm water

1/4 cup of white vinegar

1 tablespoon of baking soda

2 drops of essential oil (optional)

Then, use a clean cloth or sponge to apply the mixture to any stains or dirt on your carpets. Let the mixture sit for a few minutes before blotting it up with a clean towel.

For best results, vacuum your carpets regularly and use this DIY carpet cleaner as needed to remove any stains or dirt. With regular care, your carpets will stay looking new longer.

Having a DIY carpet cleaner on hand can be a real lifesaver when it comes to removing tough stains and dirt from your carpets. This cleaner is easy to make and requires no special skills or equipment, so you can whip it up anytime you need it. Plus, it’s made with all-natural ingredients that are fresh for your family and your pets.

If you’re looking for an easy way to keep your carpets looking new longer, then be sure to try out this DIY carpet cleaner. It’s sure to become one of your go-to cleaning solutions!
