### The Cloud Splash Park

In a city where summer temperatures often soar to 100 degrees, kids love keeping cool at community splash parks and playgrounds. Kiwanis Park in Tempe recently added a 5,000-square-foot water play structure called The Cloud that kids and parents can enjoy.

One of the design features of The Cloud, according to Wagner, is that shade structures cover almost all of the water features, shielding children from the summer sun. This makes the splash pad more enjoyable for everyone.

The splash pad also features misting sprays and a unique thunderstorm sequence. It is open daily from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m., April through October. Be sure to bring sunscreen. The 125-acre park is also home to picnic areas, four softball fields, a recreation center, a gymnasium and batting range, and 15 lighted tennis courts.

### The Kiwanis Recreation Center

The 125-acre Tempe park is a popular spot for family-oriented activities including kayak and pedal boat rentals on Kiwanis Lake, playgrounds, sports fields, batting cages, and the Cloud Splash Park. Other facilities include two large corporate picnic areas, 15 ramadas, an indoor recreation center, and a full-size gymnasium with classrooms.

NeighborhoodScout’s exclusive analysis shows that the neighbors in Kiwanis Park are lower-middle income. The most common occupations in the area are executive, management, and professional occupations.

This is a college-focused neighborhood, with more residents attending college than the average for America. A greater percentage of the population here lives in owner-occupied housing than in other neighborhoods. ### Baseball Diamonds

With five miles of walking / jogging and exercise trails, numerous ramadas, a picnic area, and the Luis Gonzalez Arizona statue, this is an ideal park for people seeking to spend leisurely afternoons with their families. The park also features a children’s playground, sports fields, and the Kiwanis Recreation Center, which houses an indoor wave pool, gym, fitness center, classrooms, and a 15-court tennis facility.

With more than 30 renovation projects under their belts, city park employees are noticing how these upgrades are improving patrons’ overall experiences at Tempe parks. Wagner says they are concentrating on improving both active and passive activities within each park. In addition, they are focusing on improving freshness and accessibility.

### Softball Fields

Known as one of the best Tempe parks, this park features baseball and softball fields, basketball courts, horseshoes, and ramadas. It also hosts numerous Ironman races and community festivals throughout the year.

The 125-acre Kiwanis Park was opened in 1972 and is named after the Kiwanis Club that donated funds for its development. Besides its sports facilities, the park boasts trees, public art, and lots of open grassy areas.

After a day of fun at this top Tempe park, you will probably work up an appetite. The good news is, the park has food amenities like grills and picnic tables. So, bring some meats and veggies for a classic summer barbecue. You can also rent pavilions for larger groups. The park has a children’s playground as well.

### The Kiwanis Tennis Center

The 15 tennis courts at this park are lighted and cushioned, and offer group and private lessons as well as leagues. The facility also features a double-sided 12-foot rebound wall, USTA staff on hand to provide information and help participants improve their game, and a digital video system to capture every point of action.

The 125-acre Kiwanis Park is also home to playgrounds, a lake, batting cages, an indoor recreation center, and a wave pool. Rent kayaks or pedal boats on the lake, and check out The Cloud, a one-of-a-kind splash park.

The Kiwanis Park neighborhood is served by the Tempe Diablos, a prestigious college tournament host team.
