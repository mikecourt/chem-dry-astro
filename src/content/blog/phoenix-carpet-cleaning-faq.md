## How does Chem-Dry carpet cleaning work?

Brimley's White Glove Chem-Dry uses a process called Hot Carbonating Extraction (HCE). Instead of soaking your carpet with hot water like traditional steam cleaning, HCE uses the natural cleaning power of carbonation — millions of tiny bubbles — to lift dirt, allergens, and bacteria from deep in your carpet fibers.

The solution we use is The Natural®, a green-certified, non-toxic hot carbonating formula. The carbonation loosens and lifts soil to the surface, where it's extracted and removed. The result is a deeper clean using **about 80% less water** than steam cleaning, with **no dirt-attracting residue** left behind.

## How long does carpet take to dry after Chem-Dry cleaning?

Most carpets are dry within **1-2 hours**. Because the Chem-Dry process uses about 80% less water than steam cleaning, there is far less moisture to evaporate. In Phoenix's warm, dry climate, carpets often dry even faster.

By comparison, steam-cleaned carpets can take 1-2 days to fully dry — a long window where wet carpet can attract dirt, develop odors, or create conditions for mold in the padding.

## Is Chem-Dry carpet cleaning safe for pets and kids?

Yes. We use The Natural®, a green-certified, non-toxic cleaning solution that contains no harsh chemicals, bleach, or heavy detergents. The Natural® is safe for children and pets. Because carpets dry in 1-2 hours, your family and pets can return to the cleaned areas the same day.

## How often should I have my carpet professionally cleaned in Phoenix?

For most Phoenix households, once or twice per year is recommended. Households with pets, children, or allergy sufferers benefit from cleaning every 6 months.

Phoenix's desert climate means fine dust and allergens accumulate year-round. The highest-value scheduling windows are:
- **January–February** — before spring allergen season
- **October** — after monsoon season and before the holidays

Carpet manufacturers typically recommend professional cleaning every 12-18 months to maintain warranty coverage.

Yes. We clean upholstered furniture including sofas, sectionals, chairs, ottomans, and other fabric pieces using the same Hot Carbonating Extraction technology adapted for upholstery fabrics. Upholstery often contains higher allergen concentrations than carpets — allergen removal from fabric is as important as carpet cleaning for overall indoor air quality.

Most upholstered pieces dry within 2-3 hours.
