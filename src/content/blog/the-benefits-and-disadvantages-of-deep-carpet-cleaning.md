### **Methods of deep carpet cleaning**

There are several methods of deep carpet cleaning. Wet cleaning and dry cleaning both use carpet-cleaning products. In both methods, the cleaning solution is sprayed onto the carpet is then sucked up with a vacuum cleaner. Dry cleaning works for water-sensitive carpets, but the carpet must be allowed to dry before a wet cleaning product can be applied. Wet cleaning can remove surface soil and stains, but dry cleaning does not remove deeply embedded dirt.

Professional portable machines are significantly more powerful than residential units. The difference between these machines is their number of vacuum motors, pumps, and heat exchangers. Professional portable machines often have two power cords, allowing them to cope with household circuits. They’re also expensive, so a professional carpet cleaner may be necessary. A deep cleaning professional will know the best way to use these machines to get the best results. If you have a very large carpet, however, you’ll need a professional machine.
### **Cost of deep carpet cleaning**

A typical carpet needs to be deep cleaned at least once or twice a year. While regular vacuuming picks up surface dirt, deep cleaning removes accumulated dust and grime. A regular cleaning schedule prevents allergies and keeps carpets soft and clean. But what is the typical cost of deep carpet cleaning? The answer varies depending on the size and type of carpet. It can range anywhere from $175 to $500, depending on the size of the home and carpet.

### **Colorfastness of carpet after deep cleaning**

If you have a brightly colored carpet, the best way to determine if your new rug will be colorfast is to test it. To do this, you will need a clean white cloth and a few drops of a carpet dye solution. Once you have the dye on your cloth, blot it with a clean white cloth. If the color remains the same after cleaning, your carpet is likely colorfast.

If you are using a spot stain remover on your carpet, it’s best to test its color fastness first. You can do this by applying a small amount to an obscure spot of the carpet and blotting with a clean, damp cloth for 10 seconds. If you see any bleeding or color change, move on to a different solution.
