Carpet cleaning is an important part of home maintenance, but it can be expensive.  There are a few things to keep in mind when using a cheap [carpet cleaner](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/). First, be sure to use the correct amount of cleaner. Too much can damage the carpet, and too little will not clean it effectively. Second, be sure to follow the instructions carefully. Some cleaners require you to rinse the area afterwards, while others do not. Finally, be sure to test the cleaner on a small area of carpet before using it on a larger area. This will help you avoid any damage to your carpet.

When using a cheap carpet cleaner, it is important to vacuum the area first. This will help remove any dirt or debris that may be trapped in the carpet. Be sure to vacuum the entire area, not just the spot where you plan to use the cleaner. After vacuuming, be sure to blot the area dry with a clean cloth. Do not rub the carpet, as this can damage it.

Once the area is dry, you can begin using the cleaner. Apply the cleaner to the affected area and scrub it in with a brush. Be sure to work the cleaner into the carpet fibers. Rinse the area well with clean water and allow it to dry completely. You may need to repeat this process several times to ensure that all of the dirt and debris are removed from the carpet.

If you find that your cheap carpet cleaner is not working as well as you hoped, there are a few things you can try. First, try using a different type of cleaner. There are many different formulas available, so find one that is specifically designed for carpets. Second, try using a higher setting on the cleaner. This will provide more power and may be more effective. Finally, if all else fails, you can always hire a professional carpet cleaner to come and clean your carpets for you.
