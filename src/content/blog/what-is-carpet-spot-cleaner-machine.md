Not only do they help to remove dirt and stains from your carpets, but they also help to keep them looking new for longer. With so many different models and brands on the market, it can be difficult to know which one is right for you. But don’t worry, we’re here to help! In this article, we’ll discuss the different features you should look for when choosing a carpet spot cleaner machine, as well as some of the best models on the market.

When choosing a [carpet spot cleaner machine](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com/appliances/g35471783/best-portable-cleaners/), the first thing you need to consider is the type of stains you want to remove. If you have light stains, then you won’t need a powerful machine. However, if you have heavy-duty stains, then you’ll need a more powerful machine that can handle them. There are also machines that are specifically designed for pet stains, so if this is a concern for you, make sure to choose one of these.

Another important factor to consider is the size of the machine. If you have a small area to clean, then you won’t need a large machine. However, if you have a large area, then you’ll need a machine that’s big enough to handle it. There are also portable carpet spot cleaners that are perfect for cleaning small areas.

Finally, you need to decide on the features you want in your machine. Some machines come with attachments that allow you to shampoo your carpets, while others have brushes that help to loosen dirt and stains. Make sure to choose a machine that has the features you need to make your life easier.
