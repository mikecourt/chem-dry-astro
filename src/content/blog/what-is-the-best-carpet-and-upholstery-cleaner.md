When it comes to [carpet and upholstery cleaning](https://pickthevacuum.com/best-carpet-and-upholstery-steam-cleaner/), there are a few different methods you can use. The most common method is vacuuming, which can remove dirt, dust, and other debris from the surface of your carpets and upholstery. However, vacuuming alone won’t remove all the dirt and grime that can build up over time. For a deeper clean, you’ll need to use a carpet and upholstery cleaner.

For a general cleaning, you can use a dry carpet and upholstery cleaner. These cleaners are typically powders that you sprinkle on the surface of your carpets and upholstery and then vacuum up. They are designed to loosen and lift dirt, dust, and other debris so that it can be easily vacuumed up.

If you’re looking for a deeper clean, you’ll need to use a foam or liquid carpet and upholstery cleaner. These cleaners are applied directly to the surface of your carpets and upholstery and then scrubbed in. They are designed to break up and remove deeply embedded dirt, dust, and grime.

When using any type of carpet or upholstery cleaner, it’s important to follow the manufacturer’s instructions carefully. Be sure to test an inconspicuous area before starting your cleaning project to make sure the chosen cleaner won’t damage the fabric. Once you’ve selected the right cleaner and cleaning method for your needs, you’ll be able to keep your carpets and upholstery looking clean and fresh for years to come.
