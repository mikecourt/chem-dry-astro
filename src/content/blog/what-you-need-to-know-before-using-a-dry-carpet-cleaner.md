Dry carpet cleaner is a product that has become more and more popular in recent years. It is a powder that is sprinkled over the carpets and then brushed in, leaving the carpets looking and smelling fresh and new. Unlike traditional carpet cleaners which use water, dry carpet cleaner does not require any wetting or rinsing of the carpets. This makes it a great choice for people who have allergies asthma, as well as for those who want to avoid having their carpets soaked for an extended period of time. [Dry carpet cleaner](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/carpet-cleaning/) is also much less likely to cause shrinkage or damage to carpets than traditional cleaners.

The benefits of using a dry carpet cleaner include:

 	- No need to rent or buy a steam cleaner

 	- Carpets dry quickly

 	- Easy to use

 	- fresh for all types of carpets

 	- Environmentally friendly

 

How to use a dry carpet cleaner:

 	- Vacuum the carpet first to remove any loose dirt.

 	- Sprinkle the dry carpet cleaner over the entire surface of the carpet.

 	- Allow the cleaner to sit for the amount of time specified on the package.

 	- Vacuum the carpet again to remove the cleaner and any dirt that has been lifted.

 

What you need to know before using a dry carpet cleaner:

 	- Read the instructions on the package thoroughly before using.

 	- Make sure the room is well ventilated while the cleaner is being used.

 	- Wear gloves and a mask if you are sensitive to chemicals.

 	- Do a small test patch in an inconspicuous area before using on the entire carpet.

Dry carpet cleaners are available in most grocery stores and hardware stores. Be sure to read the instructions on the package thoroughly before using and do a small test patch in an inconspicuous area to make sure you are satisfied with the results.
