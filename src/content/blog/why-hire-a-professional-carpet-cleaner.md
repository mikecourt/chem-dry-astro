Keeping carpets clean is important to the health of your family and guests. Regular professional cleaning removes dirt, stains, and embedded pollutants that can cause breathing problems.

### Expertise and Experience

Professional carpet cleaners have the equipment and knowledge to remove deep dirt, non-living allergens*, and stains that aren’t visible on the surface. The high-strength cleaning methods they use reach down into the fibers of your carpet and remove embedded pollutants.

Hiring a professional carpet cleaner will also save you time and hassle. They know how to tackle different types of carpets and will get the job done quickly and effectively.

Before you hire a professional, ask to see before and after photos of their work. This will give you a good idea of the quality of their work. In addition, talk to them about your budget and find out if they have any hidden charges or extra fees. Also, pay attention to how they answer the phone and whether they are friendly and helpful. If they are not, you should look elsewhere for a carpet cleaner. You deserve to have a clean home without the stress of doing it yourself!
### Advanced Odor-Fighting Techniques

Professional carpet cleaners have the advanced cleaning equipment, technology, and cleaning solutions necessary to clean deeply soiled and stinky carpets. This helps them achieve superior results in a fraction of the time it would take homeowners to do the job themselves.

Professionals can remove a wide range of stains, including pet urine, vomit, food spills, and more. They can also help prevent damp carpets from becoming musty and mildewed, which contributes to odors.

For best results, try to prevent stains from occurring in the first place by vacuuming frequently and cleaning up spills immediately. If you do have a stain, use a CRI-certified seal of approval carpet cleaning product and pretest it in an inconspicuous area to ensure it won’t damage your carpet. If possible, blot the stain rather than rubbing it to avoid pushing contaminants deeper into carpet fibers. Also, have your carpets professionally cleaned on a regular basis — every 12 to 18 months is ideal.
### Personalized Solutions

When you hire a professional carpet cleaner, they have the equipment needed to perform hot-water extraction cleaning (also known as steam cleaning). This process uses high temperatures of water to remove dirt and debris that vacuuming cannot. The professionals know how to properly set up and use this equipment for a thorough, deep clean.

They will pre-condition your carpets with a solution that loosens up the dirt. They then use a machine that sprays hot water over the entire carpet. The hot water removes the dirt and leaves the carpet looking fresh and new again.

Regular cleaning of your carpets protects your investment. It prolongs their lifespan and improves indoor air quality. Carpets trap airborne pollutants including dust mites, pet dander, and pollen. When these contaminants build up, they can cause sinus irritations and allergic reactions. Regular cleaning helps remove these contaminants and keeps your family healthy.
### Peace of Mind

A professional carpet cleaner will ensure that the job is done correctly and to your satisfaction. They will not make any shortcuts that could damage your carpet or cause a mess that you have to clean up later on. Make sure the company you hire has insurance and a satisfaction guarantee that protects you as a customer in the event something goes wrong during the cleaning process.

Dirty carpets are a breeding ground for dirt, dust mites, germs, and other unwanted organisms. These contaminants contribute to poor indoor air quality and can increase the risk of illness for family members. Professional cleaning removes these pollutants, making your home or workplace healthier and freshr for everyone.

Your carpets are a significant investment and they deserve the best care possible. Regular professional cleaning helps them maintain their high-quality look and feel for a longer period of time.
