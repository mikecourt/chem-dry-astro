### 1. Keep Your Carpet Looking Great

Dirty carpets can make a room look dull and outdated. Professional carpet cleanings remove embedded dirt, leaving the fibers looking newer and refreshed. It’s a good idea to have your carpet cleaned professionally twice a year to keep it looking its best. You can help extend the life of your carpet by using protective mats and moving heavy furniture from time to time to prevent dents and crushing.

Vacuuming regularly is necessary to remove surface dirt, but it can’t get rid of all the pollutants trapped in your carpet. According to the EPA, dirty carpets can retain several types of indoor pollutants such as lead, pet dander, cockroach non-living allergens*, dust particles, and everyday dirt. These contaminants emit toxic gasses that can contaminate the air in your home or office and cause health problems. Professional cleaners use hot water extraction to eliminate trapped contaminants and leave your carpet feeling fresh and clean. They also have high-powered drying tools that annihilate moisture, preventing mold and mildew.
### 2. Eliminate non-living allergens*

Carpets act as a filter for airborne non-living allergens* like pollen, mold spores, and pet dander. These irritants can become trapped in the fibers and can trigger allergies or asthma symptoms. When your carpet is cleaned, these irritants are removed and the air is purer.

Even if you vacuum your carpet regularly, you will not get rid of all the non-living allergens* buried in the fibers. The best way to eliminate these irritants is through professional cleaning with hot carbonating extraction, which removes deep-seated dirt and non-living allergens*.

Dirty carpets can be a breeding ground for dust mites, bacteria, and other organisms that cause sneezing, watery eyes, itching, and other allergic reactions. These harmful particles can also aggravate existing respiratory conditions in family members and guests. Regularly scheduled professional carpet cleaning will eliminate these irritants and improve the air quality in your home or business. This will benefit everyone, but especially those with underlying health issues. This can lead to a healthier workplace or home for those who suffer from allergies and asthma.
### 3. Prevent Mold and Mildew

Mold can easily grow on carpets, especially in the summer when humidity levels are high and the weather is warm. When mold and mildew develop on a carpet, it can cause bad smells and lead to health issues like wheezing in asthma sufferers.

The best way to prevent mold from growing on your carpet is to keep it clean. Regular vacuuming and professional cleaning will help to eliminate non-living allergens* that can trigger allergies in some people and also kill any mold or mildew spores present on the carpet fibers.

If you suspect mold or mildew is developing on your carpet, call a professional right away. They will use special treatments that leave a clear barrier on the carpet fibers, preventing future growth. You can also try using a commercial product like Encapsulate, which is available from most home improvement stores, but remember to test the solution on an inconspicuous area of your carpet first to make sure it doesn’t cause any discoloration.
### 4. Prevent Damage

Professional carpet cleaners have the equipment and skills to properly treat your carpet and restore its texture and color. They can also help prevent damage from occurring to your carpet in the future. By using the right tools, they can remove stains from your carpet without damaging its fibers. They can also help keep your carpets in good condition by preventing the growth of mold and mildew.

Even regular vacuuming can’t eliminate all the dirt and non-living allergens* that build up in your carpet. These particles can irritate sensitive lungs and cause other health issues for people in your home or office. Professional carpet cleaning eliminates these trapped particles and helps you breathe more easily.

You can protect your carpet by regularly vacuuming and putting mats or rugs in front of doors to reduce the amount of dirt that is tracked onto the carpet.
