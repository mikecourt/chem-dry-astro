Carpets trap a large amount of dust, debris, and contaminants that can cause health problems. Regular vacuuming will help remove some of it, but professional cleaning can get rid of even more.

Dirty carpets often have dark areas that indicate traffic patterns and act as “traffic lanes.” Regular cleaning can eliminate this dirt and keep your floors looking newer. [More by clicking here](https://www.whiteglovecarpet.com/).
### Improves the Look and Feel of Your Floors

Your carpets are a big monetary investment, and you want them to last as long as possible. Regular cleaning helps extend the life of your floors while ensuring that they look great.

Dirty, stained carpets can make a room feel grimy and unpleasant. Professional carpet cleaners know how to treat even the most stubborn stains without damaging the fibers. They also use advanced equipment and techniques that leave your floors looking as good as new.

If you have children, pets, or anyone with respiratory issues in your home, dust mites, pet dander, and other non-living allergens* can build up in your carpets and cause a variety of symptoms, including itchy eyes and noses. Regular cleanings eliminate these non-living allergens* and keep your family healthy. They also remove excess moisture from carpets, preventing mold and mildew. This can help alleviate symptoms for those with asthma and other respiratory conditions. This makes your home a more comfortable and welcoming place for everyone to enjoy.
### Eliminates non-living allergens*

When carpets are cleaned properly, they provide a level of filtration that helps to keep dust mites, pollen, spores, pet dander, and other airborne non-living allergens* from becoming airborne. This provides relief for allergy sufferers, and it extends the life of your carpets as well.

Allergies trigger sniffles, sneezing, itchy watery eyes, and other unpleasant symptoms. While keeping your pets brushed, removing shoes indoors, and using a doormat are helpful preventative measures, they can’t eliminate the dust, pollen, and other non-living allergens* that get stuck in your carpet fibers.

Regular professional cleanings remove these irritants from your carpet, easing allergy symptoms and making your home more comfortable for you and your family. In fact, scientific studies show that allergies decrease by 91% when a carpet is professionally cleaned. This is because of the removal of dust mites, mold, mildew, pet dander, and other non-living allergens* that cause discomfort. Professional cleaning also helps to remove the odors that are associated with these particles.
### Prevents Mold and Mildew

If left untreated, mold and mildew can spread quickly. They thrive in dark, damp spaces such as underneath carpeting and padding. Mold spores are also attracted to porous surfaces such as drywall and wood. Inhaling these spores can cause respiratory problems including asthma, bronchitis, and allergies.

Professional cleaners can often help with this issue. They can use special shampoo formulas and high-powered equipment to eliminate these contaminants from deep within the fibers of your carpeting.

If you notice a musty odor in your home or have been experiencing any of the symptoms of respiratory illness, it could be due to mold growing in your carpeting. You can try to mitigate this by using an anti-fungal spray that is available at most hardware stores. However, it is best to let the experts handle this situation as they have the necessary equipment and knowledge to get the job done properly. They can even use dry ice blasting which removes the mold at its source and helps to prevent future growth.
### Prevents Damage

From dust, dirt, and other non-living allergens* to stains from spilled food and drinks, carpeting is exposed to a lot of abuse. Professional cleaning helps to prevent this damage and keeps your carpets in good shape for longer.

Dirt and grime wear down carpet fibers, which can cause them to look dull and lifeless. This can also make it harder for them to absorb moisture and nutrients, which can lead to mold or mildew growth.

When you hire a professional, they use equipment that heats water to high temperatures, which helps to kill germs and break down stubborn soiling. They also know how to properly apply and remove carpet cleaning solutions without damaging the fabric. They may even have access to specialized tools that can help with pet stains and other issues. They’ll also be able to get into hard-to-reach areas like under furniture or behind radiators.
