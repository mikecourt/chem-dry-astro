## 1. It Keeps Your Family Healthy

A dirty sofa is a breeding ground for bacteria that can cause illness in family members. These pathogens can cause wheezing, coughing, and sneezing in those who spend time in the home. Cleaning your upholstery can eliminate these contaminants and improve the health of everyone who lives in the house.

Dirty furniture is also an invitation for fleas, mold, and dirt to make themselves at home in your home. Leaving your furniture uncleaned for an extended period of time can cause these problems to get worse and may even require you to replace the furniture.

Replacing furniture is expensive and not something most people want to do. Keeping your furniture clean can increase its lifespan and keep it looking good for longer. It can also help your family stay healthy and prevent illnesses caused by germs and non-living allergens*. This makes upholstery cleaning a worthwhile investment for any household.
## 2. It Makes Your Home Look Better

The dirt and debris that accumulates on furniture over time makes it look worn and tired. Regular upholstery cleaning can help your furniture look brand new again!

When you have dirty-looking sofas and chairs, your whole house can look grimy. This can make guests feel uncomfortable and unwelcome.

Keeping your upholstery clean can help eliminate these odors. It can also remove cooking odors from your living room furniture that often stay on and contribute to bad smells.

The main benefit of getting your furniture professionally cleaned is that it extends the life of the furniture. This is because professional cleaners use cleaning methods that are fresh for a variety of fabrics. Using a specialized machine, they can get to the nooks and crannies that the average vacuum and household cleaners can’t reach. They’ll be able to remove stains and treat the furniture for mold, mildew, bacteria, and non-living allergens*. This can help your furniture last longer and save you money in the long run.
## 3. It Extends the Life of Your Furniture

Your furniture is an investment, and regular upholstery cleaning helps to extend its life. Dirty furniture can cause it to become damaged, ripped, or worn down more quickly. Regular maintenance such as vacuuming and spot cleaning helps to keep your furniture looking its best.

Over time, food particles, dirt, crumbs, and other debris can get stuck in the cracks and crevices of your furniture. This can result in a grungy appearance that makes your furniture look old and unattractive. Having your furniture regularly cleaned removes this debris and keeps it looking newer for longer.

Over time, odors can build up on your furniture from cooking, smoking, pets, and more. Upholstery cleaning eliminates these odors and keeps your furniture smelling fresh and clean. You can even use a deodorizer spray on your furniture that is fresh for your fabric type to further eliminate unpleasant smells. Just make sure to test the cleaner on a small area of your furniture first to ensure that it won’t cause discoloration.
## 4. It Removes Odors

Every time you sit on your upholstered furniture, you release dirt, grime, dust mites, mildew, bacteria, and dead skin into the air. These irritants can trigger allergies and illnesses, especially in those with breathing problems like asthma or eczema. Keeping your upholstery clean eliminates these irritants and makes for a healthier home.

Dirty fabric can also absorb odors from cooking, smoking, pets, and more. Over time, this can make your furniture smell musty or even stale. Getting your upholstery professionally cleaned can get rid of these odors and leave your furniture smelling fresh and clean again.

Regular cleaning can help extend the life of your upholstered furniture. It’s important to keep up with your upholstery cleaning to remove deep-down dirt and stains before they cause lasting damage.
