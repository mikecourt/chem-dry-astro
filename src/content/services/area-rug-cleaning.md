### Professional Area Rug Cleaning

Area rugs are often cherished pieces that require special care. Whether you have a valuable Persian or Oriental rug, or a modern synthetic rug, Brimley's White Glove Chem-Dry has the expertise to clean it safely and effectively.

Our area rug cleaning process is specially designed for the unique needs of fine rugs. We can clean wool, silk, cotton, synthetic, and blended rugs with care and precision.

###### Benefits of Professional Area Rug Cleaning

- **Protects Your Investment** - Gentle cleaning preserves rug fibers
- **Color Brightening** - Brings back the vibrancy of your rug
- **Odor Removal** - Eliminates pet odors and musty smells
- **Fast Drying** - Low-moisture cleaning means quicker dry times
- **Expert Care** - Trained technicians understand different rug types

###### Our Area Rug Cleaning Process

We begin with a careful inspection to identify the fiber type, construction, and any special concerns like color bleeding or delicate fringe. Pre-treatment is applied to soiled areas, followed by our gentle Hot Carbonating Extraction that deep cleans without damage. We carefully extract dirt and moisture, then finish with grooming and optional protectant.

###### In-Home or Pick-Up Service

We can clean most area rugs in your home, or we offer pick-up and delivery service for rugs that need special attention in our facility.
