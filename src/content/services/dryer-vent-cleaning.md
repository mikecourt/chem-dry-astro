### Professional Dryer Vent Cleaning in New Lenox

K&T Chem-Dry provides professional dryer vent cleaning services to keep your home safe and your dryer running efficiently. Clogged dryer vents are a leading cause of house fires and can significantly reduce your dryer's performance.

Lint and debris build up in dryer vents over time, restricting airflow and creating a serious fire hazard. Our thorough cleaning removes all lint, dust, and debris from your dryer vent system, improving safety and efficiency.

###### Benefits of Dryer Vent Cleaning

- **Fire Safety** - Removes highly flammable lint buildup
- **Energy Savings** - Clean vents mean your dryer works more efficiently
- **Faster Drying** - Clothes dry in less time with proper airflow
- **Extends Dryer Life** - Reduces strain on your dryer's heating element
- **Prevents Mold** - Proper ventilation prevents moisture buildup

###### Signs You Need Dryer Vent Cleaning

- Clothes take longer than one cycle to dry
- Clothes are hotter than usual at the end of a cycle
- The outside of the dryer gets very hot
- You notice a burning smell when the dryer is running
- Lint or debris around the dryer vent opening
- It's been more than a year since your last cleaning

###### Our Dryer Vent Cleaning Process

We inspect your dryer vent system to assess the level of buildup and identify any issues. Using specialized equipment, we thoroughly clean the entire vent system from the dryer to the exterior vent, removing all lint, dust, and debris. We then verify proper airflow and ensure the exterior vent flap operates correctly.
