### Professional Granite Countertop Renewal in New Lenox

K&T Chem-Dry offers professional granite countertop renewal services to restore the beauty and shine of your natural stone surfaces. Our specialized process cleans, polishes, and seals your granite to protect it for years to come.

Granite countertops are a significant investment in your home. Over time, they can lose their luster due to daily use, etching from acidic substances, and wear. Our renewal process brings back the original beauty of your granite while providing lasting protection.

###### Benefits of Our Granite Renewal

- **Restores Natural Shine** - Brings back the glossy, polished look of new granite
- **Deep Cleaning** - Removes embedded dirt, oils, and stains
- **Protective Sealing** - Seals granite to resist stains and etching
- **Extends Lifespan** - Proper maintenance keeps granite beautiful longer
- **Enhances Home Value** - Beautiful countertops add to your home's appeal

###### Our Granite Renewal Process

We start with a thorough cleaning to remove all dirt, oils, and residues from the granite surface. Next, we polish the granite to restore its natural shine and remove any minor etching or dullness. Finally, we apply a professional-grade granite sealer that penetrates deep into the stone to provide long-lasting protection against stains, water damage, and etching.
