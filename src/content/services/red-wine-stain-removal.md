### Don't Let a Spill Ruin Your Evening—Or Your Carpet

Red wine spills are every homeowner's nightmare. The deep pigments in red wine—called tannins—bond quickly with carpet and upholstery fibers, creating stubborn stains that seem impossible to remove. DIY remedies like salt, club soda, or white wine often make things worse by spreading the stain or setting it deeper into fibers.

Our professional red wine stain removal service uses specialized chemistry designed specifically to break down wine tannins without damaging or bleaching your carpet or upholstery. We can treat fresh spills before they set, or tackle old wine stains that have been there for months or even years.

The key is acting fast and using the right treatment. Our technicians are trained in advanced wine stain removal techniques that work on all carpet types—wool, nylon, polyester, and even delicate natural fibers. We lift the stain without harsh scrubbing or chemicals that can damage fibers or fade colors.

###### Our Red Wine Stain Removal Process

Speed is critical with red wine stains. When you call us, we'll guide you through immediate steps to prevent the stain from setting. Our technician arrives with specialized red wine stain removal solutions that break down tannins effectively. We carefully apply the treatment, working from the outside of the stain inward to prevent spreading.
