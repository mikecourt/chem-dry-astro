### Protect Your Investment and Your Family's Health

Cleaning your carpets is only the first step. Our sanitizer and protectant services extend the life of your carpets and create a healthier indoor environment—keeping your home cleaner, longer.

###### Carpet Sanitizer

Our sanitizing treatment targets bacteria, germs, and odor-causing microorganisms that regular cleaning doesn't fully address. Applied after the Hot Carbonating Extraction process, our hospital-grade sanitizer penetrates deep into carpet fibers to eliminate the biological contaminants that affect indoor air quality.

**Ideal for:**
- Homes with young children or crawling infants
- Households with allergy or asthma sufferers
- Pet owners who want odor and bacteria control
- High-traffic areas like entryways and living rooms
- Homes with a recent illness

**Benefits:**
- Kills bacteria and reduces allergens
- Eliminates odor-causing microorganisms at the source
- Safe for children and pets after drying
- No harsh chemical residue

###### Carpet Protectant

Our protectant treatment bonds to carpet fibers, creating an invisible barrier that repels liquids and blocks dirt from penetrating deep into the pile. Think of it as a shield for your carpet—spills bead up instead of soaking in, giving you time to blot them before they become stains.

Chem-Dry protectant is applied after cleaning, when your carpet fibers are freshest and most receptive. It helps carpets resist future soiling, which means they stay cleaner longer between professional cleanings.

**Benefits of Carpet Protectant:**
- Spills bead up instead of immediately soaking in
- Makes regular vacuuming more effective
- No dirt-attracting residue—protectant won't attract new dirt
- Extends the time between professional cleanings
- Preserves carpet warranty requirements for many manufacturers
- Safe for children, pets, and allergy sufferers

###### Recommended for Every Cleaning

We recommend adding sanitizer and/or protectant to every professional cleaning. The incremental cost is minor compared to the benefit—and it's the single best way to extend the results of your cleaning and protect your carpet investment.
