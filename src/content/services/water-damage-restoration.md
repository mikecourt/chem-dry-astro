### Fast Water Damage Restoration in Denver

When water damage strikes, every hour matters. All Pro Chem-Dry provides rapid water extraction and drying services throughout the Denver metro area. Our trained technicians respond quickly to minimize damage and prevent secondary issues like mold growth.

Water damage from burst pipes, appliance leaks, flooding, or sewage backups can saturate carpets, padding, and subfloors within minutes. The longer water sits, the greater the damage—and the higher the restoration cost. That's why calling All Pro Chem-Dry immediately is critical.

###### Signs You Need Emergency Water Extraction

- Standing water or soaked carpets after a leak or flood
- Wet carpet padding that won't dry on its own
- Water intrusion from a burst pipe, appliance failure, or storm
- Musty odor that indicates moisture trapped in flooring
- Visible mold growth or discoloration on carpet or baseboards

###### Our Water Damage Restoration Process

**Step 1 — Rapid Water Extraction**
We use powerful truck-mounted extraction equipment to remove standing water fast. Our equipment removes far more water than consumer-grade wet vacuums, dramatically reducing dry time.

**Step 2 — Moisture Assessment**
We use professional moisture meters to identify all affected areas, including hidden moisture in padding, walls, and subfloors that visual inspection alone can miss.

**Step 3 — Structural Drying**
High-velocity air movers and dehumidifiers are deployed to accelerate the drying process throughout the affected area. We monitor progress and adjust equipment until moisture levels return to safe levels.

**Step 4 — Carpet and Surface Cleaning**
Once dry, we clean and sanitize affected carpets and surfaces using the Chem-Dry process to remove any contaminants introduced by the water intrusion.

**Step 5 — Final Inspection**
We verify that all areas have reached proper dry standards before completing the job—protecting your home from future mold and structural damage.

###### Why Prompt Action Prevents Bigger Problems

- **Mold can begin growing within 24-48 hours** of water intrusion
- Saturated carpet padding acts as a moisture reservoir, prolonging drying and encouraging mold
- Water that reaches subfloor or wall cavities requires far more extensive remediation
- Early extraction dramatically reduces total restoration cost
